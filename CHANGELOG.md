# Changelog

Every change to Justice League: Clawd Wars, and why.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

### Changed
- **New name and identity: Justice League: Clawd Wars.** It installs as `Justice League Clawd Wars.app` (bundle ID `com.rodclemen.justiceleagueclawdwars`, screen saver `com.rodclemen.justiceleagueclawdwars.saver`). Its hook binary is `justice-hook`; the app only ever matches, replaces or removes hook entries whose command contains that name, so other hooks are left alone. Session state lives in `~/.claude/justice-status`, the screen-saver feed in `~/Library/Application Support/Justice League Clawd Wars`, and the Copilot hook file is `~/.copilot/hooks/justice-league-clawd-wars.json`.
- **The DC cast: 110 heroes and 97 villains** in `Sources/App/Roster.swift`: the Justice League, the Bat-family, the Super-family, the Green Lantern Corps, the speedsters and Teen Titans, the Justice Society, Justice League Dark, the JLI, the Amazons, Atlanteans and New Gods, the Suicide Squad, the Legion and the Wonder Twins; against Batman's and Superman's rogues, Apokolips, the other Corps, the Flash's Rogues, Wonder Woman's and Aquaman's foes, the Legion of Doom and the League of Assassins. Krypto, Ch'p, Ace the Bat-Hound, Streaky, Gleek, Beppo, the Joker's goons, Dex-Starr and Mister Mind only ever turn up as agents. Every character's weapon, movement (flyers, hoverers, and the Bat-family swinging on grapnel lines) and shots or magic colours are set; the art is a plain stand-in until each one is drawn.
- **43 DC places** (`Sources/App/Locations.swift`, `Sources/App/Places/`), from the Watchtower and the Batcave to Apokolips, Oa and the Hall of Doom, each the home of part of the cast. Stand-in skies for now. The Watchtower is the default place.
- **Arch-enemies**: 130-odd pairs, Batman and the Joker, Superman and Lex Luthor, the Flash and Reverse-Flash, Green Lantern and Sinestro, Aquaman and Black Manta, Shazam and Black Adam and the rest, face off first whenever both are fighting.
- **Ship pickups**: the Javelin picks up Green Arrow, Black Canary and the other Leaguers who don't fly; the Batwing collects the Bat-family.
- **The Javelin and the Batwing, drawn** (`Sources/App/Art/Rides.swift`): the Javelin as a silver-and-white wedge with a big canopy, red trim, swept wings and the JL badge on its tail fin, blue thrusters; the Batwing black with a raised, scalloped bat wing carrying the yellow-oval bat, a slit canopy and an amber jet. Both keep the ramp, gear and engine states the pickups use.
- **A new app icon**: the Joker levelling his revolver at Batman under the Bat-Signal in a Gotham night sky, rendered by `Tools/MakeIcon` from the app's own sprites.
- **Superman's S-shield in the menu bar**: red S on yellow in colour; in monochrome the yellow is cut out so macOS can tint it.

- **Grapnel lines, not webs**: the swingers (Batman, Nightwing, Batgirl) hang from a thin steel-grey cable, and the engine's comments name DC characters as their examples.

### Added (scene)
- **Comic-book transitions between places** (`Sources/App/ComicWipes.swift`), in place of the Star Wars edge and clock wipes, in the window and the screen saver alike. There are 15, and a trip never repeats the last one: **Speed Force** (the old place freezes into a panel and the Flash races through it in red and yellow lightning, the picture smeared into speed lines, WHOOSH!), **Bat-Signal** (Gotham night falls, the signal lights the sky and its circle grows until the new place fills it), **Page Breaker** (the printed page strains, Superman bursts through from behind and scraps of paper fly at you, KRAASH!), **Lasso of Truth** (the golden lasso loops the panel, crumples it into a ball, flashes and whips open on the new one), **Emerald Reconstruction** (the old place dissolves into green dots, a ring's constructs frame the new place and draw it in pencil, then ink, then colour), **The Joker Card** (a giant card spins in, flips with a HAHAHA!, its back is the new place, and the rest of the deck scatters), **Atlantean Wave** (a halftone-shaded comic wave of curling breakers crashes over everything, SPLAAASH!, and recedes), **Omega Beam Fracture** (two zigzag beams burn cracks across the panel and it blows apart, KRAKOOM!), **Grappling Hook** (a grapnel catches the panel, THWIP!, and hauls it up; the new one swings down on cables and settles), **Shazam** (golden lightning, SHAZAM!, the page turns to a hard black-and-white print; a second bolt brings the new place and its colour back), **Mallet Smash** (Harley's mallet swings in, KAPOW!, and the panel shatters into falling coloured paper and confetti), **The Golden Ankh** (Doctor Fate's ankh rises in rings of glowing hieroglyphs, turns, opens like a doorway and the camera flies through), **Page Flip** (the camera pulls back to a vintage comic book, the page turns on "MEANWHILE, IN …" and the camera pushes back in), **Digital Comic Glitch** (Cyborg: the panel breaks into pixel blocks, split colour, scraps of speech balloons and blue and red circuitry, then rebuilds from pencils to full colour) and **Shattered Panels** (the panel splits into a vortex of alternate versions, tinted, inked, mirrored, in negative, and one rushes out at you as the new place). The Flash and Superman are the app's own sprites. With a transition's owners on screen (the Flash or another speedster, the Bat-family, the Super-family, the Amazons, the Lanterns, the Joker, the Atlanteans, Apokolips, Shazam or Black Adam, Harley, Doctor Fate, Cyborg) it comes up four times as often; a trip to its home turf (Gotham, Metropolis, Themyscira, Oa, Atlantis, Apokolips…) makes it three times as likely, and both six. `Tools/Transitions` renders every transition as a clip and a sheet of stills (`ONLY=2,7` for some, `FRAMES="7-wave:0.3,0.6"` for single frames).

### Fixed
- **Qwen Code and Gemini CLI hook hints name the right hook**: when either AI wasn't reporting, the help line told you to enable a hook called `clawd-wars`, but this app registers it as `justice-league-clawd-wars`.
- **Capes no longer hide legs**: standing Superman, Batman, Martian Manhunter and Shazam wore their capes in front like a skirt, covering their legs and boots. Their capes now hang down the back (Batman's with his scalloped hem, Shazam's short and gold-trimmed), the way Supergirl's and Power Girl's do; flight and swing poses are unchanged.
- **The preview no longer shows gun smoke for built-in weapons**: `build/preview cast`'s weapon-habit cell played the gun check for every ranged character, so Superman's heat vision, Green Arrow's bow or Killer Frost's bare hand blew smoke off a barrel. It now plays the bounce for `handGun: false`, as the app always did.

### Changed (code)
- **The engine speaks DC**: the weapon kinds are `.melee`, `.ranged` and `.magic`, the character mood is `CrewMode`, the ship pickup is `rideItem`/`rideExit`, the swing line is `grappleLine`, the scene floor is `sceneFloor`, the melee helpers are `drawBlade`, `swingTrail` and `meleeFirst`, and the debug switch is `CLAWD_DEBUG=1`. Comments that used other franchises' characters as examples now use DC ones, and the unused web and playing-card shot styles are gone.

### Docs (cleanup)
- **The README stands on its own**, laid out like a full product page: what it shows at a glance with the cast's signature moves, the AIs, the roster, the fights, every place and what moves there, the little things, install, build troubleshooting, how it works, uninstall and contributing.
- **History files from the apps this engine grew out of are removed**, and `llms.txt` and this changelog describe only this app.
