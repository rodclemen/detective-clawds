# What's new in Justice Clawds: Clawd Wars

The changes you'd notice in Detective Clawds, newest first. The full, detailed history lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## 3.0.0: joins Clawd Clash

- **Justice Clawds: Clawd Wars is now part of Clawd Clash**, one app with three universes: Detective Clawds, Star Clawds and Marvelous Clawds. The whole cast, every place and all fifteen comic-book transitions came along.
- **New names and redrawn emblems.** Everyone and everywhere has a parody name now (Batclaw, Superb-Man, Wondrous Woman, the Flare, the Jester, the Lookout, Gothamburg, the Night Grotto…). Superb-Man wears a C-shield, the Batclaw family a cowl mask (two pointed ears over a domino mask), the Lamplighters a lamp, the speedsters a flame; the Night Beacon lights the sky, Batclaw throws a clawarang, and the Lance's tail fin reads CL.
- **Upgrading takes care of itself.** The Homebrew cask `justice-clawds-clawd-wars` installs Clawd Clash starting in Detective Clawds; Clawd Clash takes over the AIs the old app had connected and quits the old app.
- **Pick a universe, or let it pick.** Settings → Universe switches between Detective Clawds, Star Clawds and Marvelous Clawds; switching recasts everyone on screen. **Random** changes universe on its own every 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, and in the window the whole window changes over with the new universe's transition.
- **A Settings window.** Settings… (⌘,) puts every option in one place: universe, window, desktop, which AIs are connected, the screen saver. The menu is shorter.
- **The window steps aside** when no AI is running, and fades back when one starts.
- **Keep Characters on Top**: off, the desktop crew stands just above your desktop icons and behind your windows.
- **The screen saver picks its universe** in its own Options in System Settings: the same as the app, any of the three, or Random. It also no longer drops out of System Settings' list after an update.

## 0.1.0: the first version

Where it all started, under its earlier name.

- **207 characters**: 110 heroes and 97 villains, each with its own weapon, way of moving and (nearly all) a signature move when idle.
- **43 places**, each with something moving, and the scene set where the first session's character belongs.
- **Arch-enemies face off first**, the Lance and the Clawwing pick people up, flyers take to the air, and the Batclaw family swings on grapnel lines.
- **Claude Code plays the heroes** whenever another AI (Codex, Qwen Code, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot CLI) runs alongside it.
- **Fifteen comic-book transitions** between places, in the window and the screen saver.
- **Window mode**, with a card per session, and **a screen saver** that puts on a show of its own when nothing is running.
- **A promo trailer** with its own soundtrack.
